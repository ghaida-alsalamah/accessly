# Accessly Privacy Policy

Document Status: Draft
Version: 0.1
Last Updated: 30 September 2026 by Jana

## 1. Introduction

Accessly is an AI-powered accessibility assistant that helps users identify accessibility information for events and communicate accommodation requests to event organizers in Saudi Arabia.

This Privacy Policy explains how Accessly collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data in accordance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its Implementing Regulations.

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you use Accessly, we may collect:

- Account Information: system-generated User ID, name, email address, and preferred language.
- Accessibility Information: accessibility needs and preferences, including custom needs provided by you.
- Event Information: event URL, event name, date, location, organizer name, and organizer email address.
- Request Information: request ID, request status, accessibility requirements, date the request was sent, and the status of each accessibility need.
- Authentication and Consent Information: one-time password (OTP) information, privacy consent, and consent date.
- Technical Information: session identifiers, job identifiers, usage information, and information required to operate and secure the service.

We only collect personal data that is necessary for the purposes described in this Policy.

## 3. How We Collect Your Data

We collect personal data directly from you when you:

- Provide accessibility needs or preferences.
- Submit an event or accessibility request.
- Use Accessly's features.
- Use the authentication and email functionality.

We may also collect information from event pages and communications with event organizers when necessary to provide the requested service.

## 4. Purpose of Processing

We process your personal data to:

- Analyze event accessibility information.
- Identify accessibility requirements relevant to your request.
- Prepare and send accommodation requests to event organizers.
- Track the status of requests and accessibility needs.
- Process relevant responses from event organizers.
- Authenticate users.
- Operate, maintain, secure, and improve Accessly.

Your personal data will not be processed for purposes incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected, except where permitted by applicable law.

## 5. Legal Basis

Personal data is processed based on applicable legal bases under the PDPL, including consent where required.

Where accessibility information constitutes Sensitive Data under applicable law, Accessly will apply the additional requirements and safeguards applicable to such data.

## 6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Accessly may disclose or provide access to personal data to service providers necessary to operate the service, including cloud, artificial intelligence, browser automation, and email service providers.

These providers may process personal data on behalf of Accessly only as necessary to provide their services.

Where personal data is transferred or processed outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Accessly will comply with the applicable requirements governing such transfers under the PDPL and its Implementing Regulations.

## 7. Storage, Security, and Retention

Accessly stores personal data using the systems and databases required to operate the service.

Accessly takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. When personal data is no longer necessary, it will be securely deleted or destroyed in accordance with applicable requirements.

## 8. Your Rights

Under the PDPL, you may have the right to:

- Be informed about the processing of your personal data.
- Access your personal data.
- Request a copy of your personal data.
- Request correction, completion, or updating of your data.
- Request destruction of your personal data where permitted.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

These rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions provided by applicable law.
